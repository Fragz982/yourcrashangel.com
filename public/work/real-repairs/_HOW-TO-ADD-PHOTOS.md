# Drop your real repair photos here

For each repair job you want to show off, save 3 photos into THIS folder:

  job1-checkin.jpg     <- the car when it came in (the damage)
  job1-during.jpg      <- mid-repair / teardown
  job1-after.jpg       <- finished, repaired

Next job? job2-checkin.jpg, job2-during.jpg, job2-after.jpg ... and so on.

Phone photos are fine — I'll crop/optimize them. JPG or PNG.

Then tell me:
  - "photos are in" (so I know to wire them up), and
  - for each job: the car (e.g. "2021 Honda Civic"), 1 line about the damage,
    and the final bill / estimate total (e.g. "$3,400") if you want it shown.

I'll drop them into the Before/After slider AND a new "Real Repairs" gallery on
the homepage, and use your real bills to sharpen the estimate tool's numbers.

(The gallery stays hidden until at least one job's photos are here — so nothing
looks empty in the meantime.)
